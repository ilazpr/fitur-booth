Hibird at FITUR 2027: first pass

At FITUR we present Hibird Hospitality Group as the owner and manager of hotels, venues and experiences across Puerto Rico. Because we own and manage the properties, our team at the stand is the sales team: we meet agencies, tour operators and planners, and introduce them directly to you. Below is what we have built so far. Open each one and tell us what works and what doesn't.

Property teams: find your property in Review mode (photos are numbered, and each card lists what is missing), then send Isaac what is in the list at the bottom of this page by the date he gives you. Everyone else: comment on this page or tell Isaac. Short and specific is best, for example "slide 4 is too fast" or "the Spanish line sounds odd".
For the booth TV

TV loop

Under 2 minutes, plays on repeat with no sound.

  • Is the first 20 seconds enough to make someone stop?
  • Is any text too big, too small or on screen too briefly?
  • Are the right properties and venues in the right order?
What the QR code opens

Phone page

Tap a tile and the page reorders itself. Tap any property to see its photos. The orange "Talk to us" button works from anywhere.

  • Is it clear in a few seconds who we are and what to tap?
  • Are these the right photos for your property? Review mode numbers every photo and shows what is missing.
  • Is the form short enough? Anything missing? Does the Spanish sound natural?

Property teams: what to send Isaac

Raw copy and files are fine. Do not worry about design.

  1. Photos: your 4 to 8 best, landscape, at least 1600 px wide. Use the photo numbers in Review mode to say which to replace or remove.
  2. Logo (PNG or SVG).
  3. Three highlights: short lines on what makes your property different.
  4. Rooms (count) and meeting space (room names and capacities), if you have any.
  5. FITUR offer: anything you want us to present (what, dates, conditions), or "none".
  6. Who you want to meet: types of buyers, markets, the months you need business, group sizes.
  7. Follow-up contact: name, role, email, phone or WhatsApp.
  8. Anything that reads wrong, in English or Spanish.

Send files to your property's SharePoint folder (Isaac will share the link) and text by email.
